I am a Ph.D. student at [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Hong Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2DWVrpcAAAAJ). I previously received my M.E. from University of Leeds. My research interests include **embodied AI for robotics** and **human-robot interaction**.
